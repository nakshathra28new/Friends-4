Namma 4 Peroda Story

Oru chinna journey…
oru sila memories…
oru solla mudiyadha feeling.
Konjam konjam-aa padichu paaru…
🌷

Oru chinna story…

Idhu oru normal friendship story madhiri dhaan aarambichadhu…

Aana enakku theriyama, konjam konjam-aa romba special aayiduchu.

🌱

Appo…

College-la namma meet pannom.

Starting-la namma ivlo close aaguvom nu naan nenachadhe illa.

Pesinom… sirichom… random-aa time spend pannom… apdiye days pochu.

Theriyama oru point-la… Namma 4 perum oru gang aayitom.

🫶

Aana enakku…

Enakku before-la ipdi oru proper gang irundhadhu illa.

Friends irundhanga… pesura people irundhanga… aana indha madhiri oru group-la naanum oru part nu feel pannadhu different.

Unga kooda irundhappo dhaan, “Finally… naanum oru gang-la irukken” nu feel pannen.

Adhanala dhaan neenga enakku konjam extra special.

📸

Namma little world

Indha photo paatha… enakku just oru photo madhiri theriyadhu.

Idhula irukkura 4 faces-um serndhu dhaan enakku oru complete memory.

Namma sirippu… namma conversations… namma chinna chinna moments…

Idhellam naan romba cherish panren.

✈️

Namma pesina future…

College mudinjadhuku apram kooda meet pannuvom nu pesinom.

Years later kooda serndhu iruppom nu pesinom.

Trip povom… meet pannuvom… appo kooda ipdiye pesuvom nu pesinom.

Maybe adhu just casual conversation-aa irundhirukkalam…

Aana naan adhellam nijamaave heart-la vechukitten.

🌙

Apram…

Sila vishayangal eppo maaruchu nu namakkuthaan theriyadhu.

Everything still looks normal… aana namakku mattum konjam different-aa feel aagum.

Enakkum apdi dhaan konjam konjam-aa feel aachu.

🤍

Naan edhuvum sollala…

Naan edhuvum kekkala.

Edhuvum complain pannala.

Summa normal-aa irukka try pannen.

Because… sila vishayam pesina, adhu innum awkward aagidumo nu bayam.

So naan silent-aa irundhuten.

🥺

Oru chinna aasai…

Naa text pannama irundha… konjam wait pannen.

Yaaravadhu enna thedi pesuvaangala nu…

Yaaravadhu enna nyabagam pannuvaangala nu…

Nu summa paakanum nu thonuchu.

Adhu oru test illa. Oru chinna aasai.

💭

Because…

Naan dhaan neraya times first-aa pesuren… first-aa text panren… first-aa call panren…

Adhellam naan count pannadhe illa. Because pesanum nu thonuchu.

Ungakooda pesuradhu enakku pidichirundhudhu.

Aana sila neram… naan konjam silent-aa irundhaalum, yaaravadhu enna thedi varuvaangala nu aasai vandhudhu.

🌧️

Andha silence-la…

Sila neram naan romba overthink pannen.

Maybe naan dhaan adhigamaa yosikkaren… maybe nothing changed… maybe naan dhaan sensitive…

Nu enna naane samadhanapaduthikitten.

Aana manasukkulla… konjam hurt aachu.

Sila neram azhudhen kooda.

Aana marubadiyum normal-aa irukka try pannen.

🧩

The missing piece

Oru puzzle-la oru piece konjam missing-aa irundhaalum… picture almost complete madhiri dhaan theriyum.

Aana andha piece-ku mattum… “Naan enga?” nu konjam thedal irukkum.

Maybe yaarukkum adhu theriyama irukkalam.

Maybe andha piece-um edhuvum sollama irukkalam.

Aana adhu irukkum.

Naan solla varradhu…

Idhu yaaraiyum blame panna illa.

Ungala hurt panna illa.

Neenga thappu pannitinga nu sollavum illa.

Naan feel pannadhu mattum konjam sollanum nu thonuchu.

Because… indha friendship enakku romba important.

🌷

Actually…

Namma 4 perum serndhu irundha moments-a naan miss panren.

Namma old vibe-a miss panren.

Namma random talks-a miss panren.

And maybe… namma apdiye irukkanum nu dhaan aasai.

🤍

Namma 4…

Naan perfect friendship kekkala.

Eppovume enna mattum paakanum nu kekkala.

Naan just…

Namma 4 perum serndha indha little world-a lose panna virumbala.

Neenga en life-la vandhadhu… enakku romba special.

Adhanala dhaan sila vishayam ivlo affect aagudhu.

Maybe naan solla mudiyala. Maybe naan silent-aa irundhuten. Aana naan care pannadhu mattum nijam.
